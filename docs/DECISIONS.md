Add an entry when a change reverses or extends one of these. Do not edit old entries, add a new one that supersedes them.

| D40 | 2026-11-12 | `Poptat spolupráci` on an offer is an invitation from the viewer (poster V) to the offerer (O), not interest from O's side. V sends one of his own active listings that fit the offer (`offerFitsJob` in `src/lib/matching.ts`) or creates a new direct listing sent only to O (`jobs.visibility = 'direct'`, `jobs.direct_to`, not on the board, shown in V's `Moje inzeráty` as `Jen pro <name>`). O has `INVITE_RESPOND_DAYS = 3` days (`engagements.respond_by`, `initiated_by = 'poster'`); after that the invitation is `Nepotvrzeno`, a DERIVED state (pending and `respond_by` < today, no new status). O still sees it in `Poptávky pro mě` with V's contacts and keeps the notification (notifications are never auto-deleted). V can re-send (new 3 days), publish a direct listing on the board (`PATCH visibility: 'public'`, then the usual matching notifications) or end it. Declined interest (either side) is hidden from lists, not deleted; re-responding after `declined` stays 409 (`Zadavatel už vybral jinak.` for a worker, `Tuhle poptávku už odmítl.` for an invitation); a NEW direct listing for the same offerer stays allowed. One confirmable direct invitation per person at a time (second `directOfferId` → 409 `Už mu jedna poptávka čeká na potvrzení.`). Publishing (`PATCH visibility: 'public'`) is refused while a confirmable invitation waits (409 `Pozvánka ještě čeká na potvrzení.`) or after it was accepted (`Pozvaný už potvrdil.`), notifies matches except the invited person, and the owner's `PublicJob.directInvite` carries the state. Re-opening a withdrawn row via `respond` resets `initiated_by` to `worker` and `respond_by` to NULL. A poster cancelling a pending invitation sets `withdrawn` (so a re-invite is possible) and notifies O (`invite_cancelled`). `POST /api/jobs/:id/respond` on an offer is 409 `Použij Poptat spolupráci.`; legacy worker-initiated engagements on offers still display and can be declined/withdrawn. Migration 0014: `engagements.respond_by`, `jobs.direct_to`, and a rebuild of `notifications` because the `type` CHECK had to allow `invited`, `invite_accepted`, `invite_declined`, `invite_cancelled`. | Owner decision: the viewer who wants to cooperate must offer a concrete job, and an unanswered invitation must not block anyone. | owner |
| D41 | 2026-11-13 | New optional step `Kontakt navázán` between `pending` and `accepted`, for worker interest and poster invitations. Either side confirms it with one click (`POST /api/engagements/:id {action:'contact'}`); the other side gets a `contacted` notification. Contact cancels the 3-day invitation deadline (a contacted invitation is never `unconfirmed`; contact on an already unconfirmed invitation revives it). After contact either side can end it with `Nedohodli jsme se` (`{action:'no_deal'}`): worker maps to `withdrawn`, poster to `declined` (on a poster invitation always `withdrawn`, so a re-invite stays possible), the row stays, the list entry is hidden like a decline, the other side gets `no_deal`. Accept stays possible directly from `pending`. The state is derived (`status = 'pending' AND contacted_at IS NOT NULL`, columns `contacted_at`, `contacted_by`), the `engagements.status` CHECK is unchanged. Re-opening (worker respond, re-invite) clears `contacted_at`. Poster cancel of a contacted invitation is 409 (use `no_deal`). Migration 0015 rebuilds `notifications` WITHOUT the `type` CHECK and adds `notifications.actor_id` (who confirmed contact or ended it; the notification shows that name); types are validated in code by the `NotificationType` union. The new types are not in the e-mail digest. | Contact is often established by phone before anyone accepts; the app should record it, stop the invitation clock, and let a failed negotiation end cleanly. A CHECK on `type` forced a table rebuild for each new notification type (0013, 0014); validating in code avoids that. | owner |
